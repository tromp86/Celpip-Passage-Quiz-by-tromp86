How are questions & texts picked?

The texts here are written at a CELPIP 7–9 level — a good challenge if you want to sharpen your reading and push your score higher.

One text is active at a time. Its 5 questions are put into a shuffled queue, and each "Next" pulls the next item from that queue — so within a single run, no question repeats until all 5 have been asked.

When a run finishes (or you hit "Switch to another text"), a new text is chosen from a separate shuffled queue of all the texts, with one rule: it can't be the same text that was just active. If that text comes up again later, its first question also won't repeat the one it ended on last time.

As a result:

  • All 5 questions of a text are asked once each, in a random order, before you see the score.
  • The next text is always different from the one you just finished — never back-to-back.
  • You can abandon a text early with the switch button, and pick up a different one immediately.
Questions never repeat right away — each text has its own queue of 5 questions, which is only reshuffled once all of them have been shown. The order of the texts is randomized every round as well.
