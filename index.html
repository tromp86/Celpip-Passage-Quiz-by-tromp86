How are questions & texts picked?

The texts here are written at a CELPIP 7–9 level — a good challenge if you want to sharpen your reading and push your score higher.

For each text we keep our own queue of indices into its 5 questions, shuffled randomly. Every time a question for that text is needed, we take the next item from its queue. Once the queue runs out (all 5 shown), it's reshuffled, but with one rule: the first question of the new queue can't be the same as the last question of the previous queue.

The same idea is used for the order of the texts: instead of always starting at text 1, we shuffle the order of all texts into a queue and pull from it round after round, with the same no-repeat-at-the-boundary rule.

As a result:

  • A given text's question never repeats until the other 4 have all been shown.
  • The same question never appears twice in a row, even across a reshuffle boundary.
  • The order the texts appear in is randomized every round, and never repeats the same text back-to-back.
Questions never repeat right away — each text has its own queue of 5 questions, which is only reshuffled once all of them have been shown. The order of the texts is randomized every round as well.
